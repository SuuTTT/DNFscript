Aetherfold Arena

A small original card-duel playground for practising turn order, hand choices, lane pressure, and deterministic opponent logic.

Original mock only. This is not a commercial game client or recreation. It uses original card names and rules, stores only synthetic state in this browser, and has no account, capture, input bridge, or external-game adapter.

How to play

1. Select a card in your hand.
2. Click an empty friendly lane to deploy it.
3. On your next turn, click a ready unit to attack the rival in the same lane—or its core if that lane is clear.
4. End the round to let the deterministic practice rival take its turn.
